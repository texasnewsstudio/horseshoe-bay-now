What area producers should know

Livestock prices vary by weight, condition, breed, health, weaning history, lot size and sale location. The statewide weighted averages are useful for broad market context, but they are not a guaranteed price at any one sale barn. Local receipts and price ranges can differ materially from the state aggregate.

The latest published digest reported moderate-to-good demand and noted that feeder steers and heifers were mostly lower in the week. Texas sale reports are published on different schedules; where an individual Central Texas auction report or hay quote was not verified for this snapshot, no local price is substituted.
