Meet Mark Meyers

Born and raised in Dallas, Mark developed an early fascination with thunderstorms, tornadoes, and the way severe weather can change by the minute.

In 1982, he bought a Bearcat 20/20 scanner to follow storm reports and radio communications. That interest stayed with him through a career in technology and broadcasting.

Although he once imagined becoming a television meteorologist, his path led to founding Texas News Studio, where he occasionally shares live weather observations and nowcasting during developing Texas weather.

Today he follows the weather around Horseshoe Bay and Lake LBJ — from summer heat and lake breezes to sudden downpours and fast-moving Hill Country storms.

Important: Mark shares weather observations as an enthusiast, not a credentialed meteorologist. For official warnings, watches and advisories, rely on the National Weather Service.
