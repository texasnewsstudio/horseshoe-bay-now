CANYON LAKE REMAINS NEAR FULL AS MORE RAIN APPROACHES

A broader Hill Country water story: Canyon Lake stood at roughly 96.7 percent capacity Friday.

HORSESHOE BAY NOW · Source reporting: MySA · November 14

Canyon Lake was reported at about 96.7 percent of capacity on November 13, substantially higher than at the same point last year.

The reservoir's levels are a useful regional comparison, but Canyon Lake is not part of the Highland Lakes chain. Additional weekend rain could affect watershed runoff; actual lake levels can change.
