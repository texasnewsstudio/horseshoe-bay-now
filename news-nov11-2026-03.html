COTTONWOOD SHORES SEEKS INFRASTRUCTURE GRANT

The city is seeking up to $750,000 in state funding for infrastructure improvements; an application is not an award.

Cottonwood Shores is pursuing up to $750,000 in state grant funding for infrastructure improvements, according to regional reporting published November 10. The proposal remains a funding request, not a confirmed grant award. Residents can follow city meeting materials for project scope and decisions.
