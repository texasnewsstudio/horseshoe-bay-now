COTTONWOOD SHORES PLANS TWO OUTDOOR EMERGENCY SIRENS

The lakeside city is moving toward a new way to alert residents outdoors.

Cottonwood Shores is installing two outdoor warning sirens intended to alert people to severe weather and other emergencies, local reporting said November 11. Sirens can be especially helpful at parks and outdoor gathering areas, but they are not a substitute for phone alerts, weather radio or official emergency instructions. Residents should watch for the city's installation and testing announcements.
