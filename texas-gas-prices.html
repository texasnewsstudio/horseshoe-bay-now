Why it matters in the Highland Lakes

Fuel prices affect household travel, lake recreation, contractors, delivery costs and the operating expenses of local businesses. The Austin–San Marcos and Killeen–Temple–Fort Hood metro figures provide nearby regional reference points, but they are not a separately published Horseshoe Bay average.

AAA updates its retail averages daily. Prices at individual stations can be higher or lower, and the national diesel value was not displayed in the retrieved AAA snapshot, so it is shown as a dash rather than estimated. Use the linked AAA source for the latest daily readings and local station prices.
