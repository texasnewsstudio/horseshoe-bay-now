HORSESHOE BAY UTILITY RATE CHANGES WILL FIRST APPEAR ON DECEMBER BILLS

November's adopted increases affect water, sewer and garbage charges.

Horseshoe Bay's utility rate adjustments took effect November 1, but the city says residents will first see the changes on their December 2026 bills because charges are billed after usage. The change covers water, sewer and garbage service, with the actual impact varying by meter size and water consumption.

The city lists a sewer base-rate increase from $60.64 to $70.64, while tiered water-volume rates rise 3 percent. Garbage service rates increase 5.9 percent. For typical homeowners, the city estimates total monthly bill increases ranging from 9 to 17.9 percent depending on meter size and usage.

City officials say the water and sewer increases support capital improvements and system needs. Residents comparing bills should check their meter size, consumption and billing period instead of assuming every household will see the same dollar increase.
