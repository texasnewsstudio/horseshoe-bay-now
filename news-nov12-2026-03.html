BRIGGS-BASED FIREFLY AEROSPACE LANDS $1.3 BILLION DEAL

A new contract with AST SpaceMobile puts a Highland Lakes-area aerospace company in the spotlight.

Firefly Aerospace, based in Briggs, has secured a reported $1.3 billion contract with fellow Texas company AST SpaceMobile. The November 11 announcement underscores the role of the rural Highland Lakes region in the expanding space industry. The contract is a significant business development, although the reporting does not establish how many local jobs or facilities may result.

Source: DailyTrib.com local reporting, November 10–11, 2026.
