SUPERINTENDENT ON THE MOVE? BURNET ISD LEADER EYES ANOTHER DISTRICT

BCISD says its superintendent is pursuing a position elsewhere.

Burnet Consolidated Independent School District announced November 18 that its superintendent is pursuing a position with another school district, according to reporting published November 19.

Exploring another role is not the same as a confirmed resignation or a completed appointment. Families and employees should watch the district for official updates.

This article reflects the reported development as of November 20 without predicting the outcome of the hiring process.
