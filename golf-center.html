Golf Guide & Frequently Asked Questions

Can visitors play at Horseshoe Bay Resort?

Slick Rock, Apple Rock and Ram Rock offer resort golf access subject to eligibility, lodging arrangements and course policies. Summit Rock is a members-only course. Verify current access with the resort.

Which course is best for scenic views?

Apple Rock is particularly known for elevated Hill Country and Lake LBJ views. Summit Rock also offers dramatic panoramas, but is private.

Which course is most challenging?

Ram Rock is widely described as the resort's toughest Robert Trent Jones Sr. course, with tight fairways and a higher published course rating than Slick Rock.

When is golf weather best?

Morning rounds can reduce summer heat exposure, but check live NWS hourly forecasts for wind, rain, and thunderstorm risks before leaving.

Is there public golf in Horseshoe Bay?

Blue Lake Golf Club offers a public nine-hole option. Access to resort and private courses differs; confirm before visiting.

What does the golf outlook mean?

It is a simple weather-based estimate calculated from forecast temperature, wind and rain chance. It does not measure actual course condition, pace of play, or tee-time availability.
