CITY'S WATER WINS TEXAS HONOR! HORSESHOE BAY TOUTS TOP-TASTING TAP

City highlights a 2026 statewide water-quality recognition.

The City of Horseshoe Bay's official website lists a 2026 Best Tasting Water in Texas award for its Utilities Department.

The honor recognizes the city's water in a tasting competition; it should not be interpreted as a real-time laboratory report or a guarantee that individual plumbing systems have identical water quality.
