HORSESHOE BAY ANNOUNCES ENGINE 11 FIRE DEPARTMENT CEREMONY

A new city alert invites residents to a Fire Department Engine 11 push-in ceremony.

HORSESHOE BAY NOW · Source reporting: City of Horseshoe Bay · November 13

The City of Horseshoe Bay posted a public alert on November 13 inviting the community to a Fire Department Engine 11 push-in ceremony.

The city notice is an invitation, not confirmation that the event has already taken place. Residents should consult the official alert for ceremony details and any changes.
