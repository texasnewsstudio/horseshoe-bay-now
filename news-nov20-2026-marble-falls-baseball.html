MARBLE FALLS SCHOOLS · NOVEMBER 20, 2026 · LOCAL NEWS DESK

$8 MILLION BALLPARK NEARS FINISH LINE! MUSTANGS EYE 2027 DEBUT

Marble Falls High School's rebuilt Scearce Field is expected to be ready for the new baseball season.

The revamped Scearce Field at Marble Falls High School is nearing completion, with the district targeting December 31 for the work to finish.

The approximately $8 million field is part of the district's larger 2025 bond program. The project includes turf, dugouts, bleachers and a clubhouse.

District officials expect the Mustangs to be able to use the field after winter break, ahead of the 2027 baseball season.
