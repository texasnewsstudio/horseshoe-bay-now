BURNET SPARK TANK RAISES OVER $70,000 FOR CLASSROOM IDEAS

An education foundation fundraiser will help teachers bring new projects to students.

The Burnet Consolidated ISD Education Foundation raised more than $70,000 at its second annual Spark Tank fundraiser, according to a November 10 report. The program supports teachers proposing innovative classroom projects. The fundraising total reflects local interest in school programs beyond regular district funding.
