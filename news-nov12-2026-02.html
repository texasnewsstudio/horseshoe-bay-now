MARBLE FALLS HOSPITAL SETS EMERGENCY RESPONSE DRILL

Baylor Scott & White Medical Center plans a mass-casualty exercise for November 12.

Baylor Scott & White Medical Center in Marble Falls has scheduled a mass-casualty drill for Thursday, November 12, according to a November 11 report. The exercise is designed to test emergency-response readiness. It is a planned drill, not a report of an actual mass-casualty incident. Residents who notice unusual emergency activity around the hospital should distinguish the exercise from a real event.
