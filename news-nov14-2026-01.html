THREE FUGITIVES ARRESTED IN MARBLE FALLS

Police and a fugitive task force arrested three men on outstanding warrants during a Thursday operation.

HORSESHOE BAY NOW · Source reporting: DailyTrib · November 13

Marble Falls police, assisted by the Lone Star Fugitive Task Force and Burnet police, made three arrests on November 12 at a residence on Amy Circle, according to local reporting published November 13.

The men faced separate outstanding warrants. Arrests and charges are allegations, not findings of guilt. The case is a reminder of the continuing work by regional law-enforcement agencies.
