FIVE MARBLE FALLS SENIORS CELEBRATE COLLEGE SPORTS SIGNINGS

Published November 15, 2026 · Horseshoe Bay Now

Five Marble Falls High School athletes marked a major milestone in their athletic careers.

A November 13 local report identified Madison Cuplin, Sidney Solomon, Amaris Mitchell-Ochoa, Jocelyn Suarez and Addie Moomaw among the seniors recognized at a college sports signing ceremony.

Signing ceremonies celebrate years of practice and the support of coaches, teachers and families. They also provide younger athletes with visible examples of opportunities beyond high school.
