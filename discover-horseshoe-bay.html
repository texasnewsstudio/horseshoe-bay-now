Discover Horseshoe Bay

Lake LBJ, championship golf, relaxing getaways and the best of Hill Country living—all in one easy-to-read guide.

Welcome to the lake

A special place in the Texas Hill Country

Horseshoe Bay sits along Lake LBJ, west of Marble Falls in the Texas Hill Country. Known for waterfront scenery, golf and a relaxed resort atmosphere, the community draws residents, weekend visitors and vacationers who want time outdoors.

Whether you are visiting for a day or considering a longer stay, here's a practical introduction to the area's best-known experiences.

Explore the area

Fairways & views

Golf

Horseshoe Bay is closely associated with golf, including the well-known Slick Rock, Ram Rock and Apple Rock courses at Horseshoe Bay Resort. Each offers a different style of play, from rolling terrain to demanding layouts and striking Hill Country views. Access, tee times and guest eligibility can vary, so check directly with the resort before planning a round.

On the water

Lake LBJ & Boating

Lake LBJ is a central part of life around Horseshoe Bay. Boating, fishing, paddling and waterfront relaxation bring visitors to the area throughout much of the year. The lake is generally managed near a consistent level, but conditions, access and boating safety can change. Check current weather, lake information and launch access before heading out.

Stay a while

Lodging & Resorts

Visitors can choose from resort accommodations, vacation homes and other lodging in the Horseshoe Bay and Marble Falls area. Some travelers come for golf and spa amenities; others prefer a lakeside rental for a family getaway. Compare location, waterfront access, parking, pet policies and any resort restrictions before booking.

Relax & dine

Dining

Dining around Horseshoe Bay ranges from resort restaurants to casual spots in nearby Marble Falls and surrounding communities. A relaxed meal after golf or boating is part of the appeal. Some resort dining may have access restrictions or require reservations, so verify hours and availability before visiting.

Local essentials

Shopping & Services

Horseshoe Bay offers neighborhood conveniences and services, while nearby Marble Falls provides a wider selection of groceries, shopping and everyday errands. Visitors planning an extended stay may find it useful to shop for supplies before a full day on the lake.

Beyond the shoreline

Things to Do

Beyond golf and boating, the surrounding Hill Country offers scenic drives, parks, sightseeing and small-town exploring. Marble Falls and the wider Highland Lakes region make easy additions to a Horseshoe Bay visit. Outdoor plans are best matched to the season, especially during hot summer afternoons or severe weather.

Visitor note: Resort access, business hours, lake conditions and activity availability should be confirmed before visiting.
