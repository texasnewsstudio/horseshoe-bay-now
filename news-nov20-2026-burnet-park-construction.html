$1.65 MILLION PARK REBUILD! BURNET CREWS ROLL INTO ACTION

Galloway-Hammond Park is getting a new concession and seating hub after flood damage.

Construction has started on a $1.65 million concession stand and seating hub at Burnet's Galloway-Hammond Park, replacing a structure damaged in the July 2025 flooding.

The city expects the project to be completed around summer 2027. Planned amenities include restrooms, covered seating and an air-conditioned concession area.

Baseball and softball fields are expected to remain accessible during construction, but visitors should respect fencing and work-zone barriers.
