Understanding market updates

Are the quotes real-time? Some cryptocurrency and currency quotes may update quickly, while stock and exchange data may be delayed. TradingView determines available feeds and timing.

Why are markets sometimes unchanged? U.S. stock exchanges have regular sessions and holiday closures. Crypto markets operate around the clock, but displayed data can still be delayed by the provider.

Does a rising index mean every stock is rising? No. Market indexes represent groups of securities; individual companies and sectors can move in different directions.

How often does the dashboard change? The supplied dashboard rotates its asset boards and featured market views automatically, while connected third-party widgets supply market information.
