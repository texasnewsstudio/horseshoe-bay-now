ROAD FUTURE ON THE LINE! BURNET COUNTY ASKS PUBLIC TO SPEAK UP

Residents have opportunities to weigh in on transportation infrastructure priorities.

Burnet County commissioners have scheduled public meetings to hear community input on future transportation infrastructure, according to local reporting published November 13.

Road capacity, safety and travel patterns are practical issues for a county experiencing continued growth. Public meetings allow residents to describe the improvements they believe matter most.

This November 20 report is a planning preview, not an announcement that any particular road project has been approved or funded.
