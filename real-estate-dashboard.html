Horseshoe Bay real estate: neighborhoods and local market guide

These measures describe different parts of the market and should not be treated as interchangeable.

Slick Rock and golf-course living

Slick Rock is one of Horseshoe Bay’s established golf-oriented residential areas. Compare property type, lot characteristics, association obligations and recent comparable sales before interpreting citywide asking prices for an individual home.

Horseshoe Bay West

Horseshoe Bay West includes residential areas with different property sizes and access to resort-area amenities. Review the neighborhood comparison table above for published listing-price coverage, and check the reporting period before drawing conclusions.

Applehead Island and waterfront property

Waterfront homes can differ sharply from inland properties because of lake frontage, docks, views and individual improvements. A broad Horseshoe Bay median is not a reliable estimate of an Applehead Island or Lake LBJ waterfront home.

Marble Falls, Kingsland and nearby communities

Horseshoe Bay housing market questions

What is the typical home price in Horseshoe Bay?

The answer depends on the measure. Zillow’s typical home value is an estimate of the housing stock; median asking price describes active listings; median sale price describes homes that actually closed. The dated figures above identify each measure and its source.

Are Horseshoe Bay home prices going up or down?

Check the year-over-year change and publication month in the market cards. Short-term movements can reflect which properties sold or were listed, particularly in a market with a wide range of luxury and smaller homes.

How can I compare Slick Rock and Horseshoe Bay West?

Start with the neighborhood comparison table, then compare similar property types, recent transactions and carrying costs. Where a neighborhood statistic is unavailable, do not substitute a citywide figure as though it were neighborhood-specific.

Is the dashboard a live MLS feed?

No. It displays dated public market snapshots and links to the original research providers. Published statistics are updated on different schedules and are not live individual-property listings.
