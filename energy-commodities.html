What the markets show

Compare average regular gasoline, premium gasoline and diesel prices in Texas and other states. These are statewide averages, not individual station prices.

Crude oil and natural gas

Follow WTI and Brent crude, natural gas and related energy contracts. Futures prices differ from what consumers pay at the pump.

Gold, silver and copper

Precious and industrial metals respond to economic activity, inflation expectations and global supply and demand.

Agricultural commodities

Track corn, wheat, soybeans, coffee, sugar and livestock contracts. Prices can be quoted in cents per bushel, cents per pound or other contract units.

Why energy prices matter in the Texas Hill Country

Gasoline and diesel costs affect commuting, boating, deliveries and travel around Horseshoe Bay, Marble Falls and the Highland Lakes. Oil and natural gas prices also influence the broader Texas economy. The prices shown here are market information, not personalized investment advice.

Frequently asked questions

Are the gas prices current?

Not necessarily. The Yahoo Finance quote panel above checks for updates hourly. The supplemental dashboard may show unavailable prices when its sources fail; always check the displayed quote timestamp.

Are commodity prices the same as retail prices?

No. Futures, spot quotes and broker-indicative prices can differ from retail fuel prices and from official exchange settlements.
