How to read the allocations

Monthly allocations are payments distributed to local taxing jurisdictions, not a direct measure of all taxable sales occurring inside a city during that same calendar month. The Comptroller notes that allocations generally reflect tax collections from sales made two months or more before the payment date. Monthly amounts can also be affected by reporting timing and adjustments.

For a fair comparison, review the current payment, the comparable payment from the same month in the prior year, and year-to-date totals. Do not interpret one month's change as a complete measure of local business health.
