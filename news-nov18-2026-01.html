HORSESHOE BAY RESIDENTS ASKED TO HELP SHAPE CITY'S COMPREHENSIVE PLAN

The city is inviting residents to review planning material and complete a survey.

Horseshoe Bay is asking residents to participate in a review of its comprehensive plan, according to an announcement on the city's official website. The public invitation includes reviewing planning information and responding to a survey, giving people who live in the city an opportunity to contribute their priorities.

A comprehensive plan is a guide for future land use and community decisions, not an immediate change to zoning or a guarantee that a particular project will be built. Residents can use the process to identify concerns such as traffic, neighborhood character, services and long-term growth.

The city's notice is a call for input rather than a report that a final plan has been adopted. Residents should use the city's official website for the survey, materials and any posted deadline.
