COTTONWOOD SHORES PLANS TWO EMERGENCY WARNING SIRENS

Two outdoor sirens are planned to warn residents about severe weather and other emergencies.

Cottonwood Shores is preparing to install two outdoor emergency warning sirens, according to local reporting published November 11. The sirens are intended to alert people outdoors to threats including tornadoes, severe thunderstorms, flooding and other public-safety emergencies. Installation is expected by the end of 2026. Residents should continue using official alerts and weather information rather than relying on sirens alone.

Source: DailyTrib.com local reporting, November 10–11, 2026.
