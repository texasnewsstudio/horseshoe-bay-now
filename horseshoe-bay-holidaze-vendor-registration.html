HoliDaze approaches: vendors invited to sign up

The city is promoting the Business Alliance event scheduled for December 1.

What residents should know

A November 24 municipal alert says vendors can still sign up. Businesses should check the organizer for current space availability, fees and setup arrangements. The city also promoted the event in a November 20 notice.
