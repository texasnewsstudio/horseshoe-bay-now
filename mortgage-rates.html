What this means locally

Mortgage rates influence monthly payments, buyer purchasing power and refinancing calculations across Horseshoe Bay and the Highland Lakes. Freddie Mac’s survey reflects a defined national loan sample; it is not a lender quote for a particular borrower, property, down payment or credit profile.

For local context, compare these national averages with the housing inventory, asking prices and market activity in the Horseshoe Bay Real Estate Dashboard. Rates can change between weekly survey dates, and individual offers may differ because of points, fees, loan size, property type and borrower qualifications.

Data source and reporting date: Freddie Mac Primary Mortgage Market Survey, November 5, 2026. Survey averages are for conventional conforming fully amortizing home-purchase loans; they are not a personalized quote.
