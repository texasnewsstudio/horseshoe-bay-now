BURNET MARCHING BAND CLOSES ITS SEASON ON THE STATE STAGE

The Esprit de Corps concluded its competitive marching season at state competition.

Burnet High School's Esprit de Corps marching band wrapped up its competitive season at the state contest, according to November 12 reporting. The achievement reflects months of rehearsals and a major commitment from students, directors and families. The performance is a reminder that high-school achievement in the Highland Lakes extends well beyond athletics.
