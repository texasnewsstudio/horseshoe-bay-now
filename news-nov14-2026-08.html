FIVE MARBLE FALLS SENIORS SIGN FOR COLLEGE SPORTS

Five high-school athletes celebrated their commitments to compete at the next level.

HORSESHOE BAY NOW · Source reporting: DailyTrib · November 13

Marble Falls High School recognized five seniors signing to compete in college athletics, according to November 13 coverage.

The signing group includes Madison Cuplin, Sidney Solomon, Amaris Mitchell-Ochoa, Jocelyn Suarez and Addie Moomaw. The achievement highlights the school's athletics programs and the students' next steps.
