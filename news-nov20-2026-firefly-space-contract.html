SPACE INDUSTRY STUNNER! FIREFLY'S $1.3 BILLION DEAL PUTS BRIGGS ON MAP

A reported major agreement with AST SpaceMobile shines a spotlight on the region's aerospace sector.

Briggs-based Firefly Aerospace has announced a major agreement with AST SpaceMobile, reported at approximately $1.3 billion.

The deal draws attention to Burnet County's connection with the aerospace industry and the potential long-term significance of large commercial space contracts.

A contract announcement is not proof that the entire amount has been received or that a specific number of local jobs has been created. Those outcomes depend on execution and subsequent disclosures.
