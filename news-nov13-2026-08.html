MARBLE FALLS HOSPITAL HOLDS EMERGENCY RESPONSE EXERCISE

Baylor Scott & White scheduled a mass-casualty drill for November 12.

HORSESHOE BAY NOW · Original source report: November 11, 2026

Baylor Scott & White Medical Center in Marble Falls scheduled a mass-casualty response exercise for November 12. Such drills help hospitals and emergency partners practice coordinating care during major incidents. The reported event was a planned exercise, not a real mass-casualty emergency.
