LAKE LBJ: DAM OPERATIONS REMAIN A KEY WATCH POINT

Published November 15, 2026 · Horseshoe Bay Now

LCRA's latest flood update says no floodgate operations are expected at Wirtz Dam.

The Lower Colorado River Authority's November 15 early-morning status lists no expected gate operations at Wirtz Dam on Lake LBJ or Starcke Dam on Lake Marble Falls. That follows a period of elevated river flows earlier in the week.

Lake LBJ is a pass-through lake, so rainfall upstream can affect conditions faster than on reservoirs designed for flood storage. Boaters and waterfront residents should monitor LCRA's live Hydromet readings because operating conditions can change.
