WIRTZ DAM BRIDGE PROJECT RETURNS TO THE SPOTLIGHT

An updated report revisits the long-discussed bridge project affecting Highland Lakes communities.

A November 12 local report provides an update on the Wirtz Dam bridge project after years of planning, revisions and adjustments.
