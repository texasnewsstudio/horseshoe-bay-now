FIREFLY AEROSPACE'S $1.3 BILLION DEAL PUTS THE HILL COUNTRY IN ORBIT

The Briggs-based company announced a major contract with AST SpaceMobile.

Briggs-based Firefly Aerospace has secured a reported $1.3 billion contract with AST SpaceMobile, according to local coverage published November 11. The agreement highlights how advanced aerospace manufacturing is becoming part of the wider Highland Lakes economy. A contract announcement does not mean the full value has already been earned or that local hiring numbers have been confirmed.
