HORSESHOE BAY CITY OFFICES CLOSED FOR VETERANS DAY

The city announced a Veterans Day closure for Wednesday, November 11. Residents should check city channels for service updates.

The City of Horseshoe Bay posted a notice on November 10 that city offices would be closed for Veterans Day, Wednesday, November 11, 2026. Residents needing city services should consult the city's official alerts and contact information before visiting. The closure notice does not establish changes to emergency response or every municipal service.
