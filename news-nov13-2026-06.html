BURNET SPARK TANK RAISES MORE THAN $70,000 FOR CLASSROOMS

A community fundraiser will help teachers develop new classroom projects.

HORSESHOE BAY NOW · Original source report: November 10, 2026

Burnet's Spark Tank fundraiser raised more than $70,000 to support classroom innovation, according to local reporting. The money is intended to help educators put creative projects and learning opportunities into practice. The result highlights continued community support for area schools.

Reporting reference: DailyTrib.com — local coverage. This article is a November 13 roundup of previously reported developments.
