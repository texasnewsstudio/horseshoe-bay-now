MARBLE FALLS TREE LIGHTING SET FOR FRIDAY EVENING

The city plans to light its large Christmas tree at Harmony Park around 6 p.m. November 13.

Marble Falls is preparing to switch on its large community Christmas tree at dusk on Friday, November 13. Local reporting places the lighting at about 6 p.m. in Harmony Park. The event marks the start of another holiday season downtown. Visitors should check city announcements for any schedule or parking changes before heading out.
