BURNET TRAIL IMPROVEMENTS MOVE CLOSER TO PUBLIC USE

Published November 15, 2026 · Horseshoe Bay Now

Concrete paving at Burnet's Haley Nelson Park walking trail has recently wrapped up.

Local reporting published November 13 says the walking trail paving project at Haley Nelson Park is nearing completion. For walkers and families, a finished paved route can improve accessibility and create another option for outdoor recreation.

Paving completion does not necessarily mean every project element is finished or that the full route is open. Visitors should check city updates for access and any remaining work before planning a trip.
