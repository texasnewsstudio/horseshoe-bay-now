COTTONWOOD SHORES SEEKS $750,000 FOR INFRASTRUCTURE

A state grant application could support local improvements if funding is awarded.

Cottonwood Shores is pursuing up to $750,000 in state grant assistance for infrastructure work, according to reporting published November 10. The proposal reflects the cost of maintaining and improving city systems. The money has been requested, not awarded, and any project timetable depends on the application outcome.

Source: DailyTrib.com local reporting, November 10–11, 2026.
