Low-water crossing faces extended closure

Construction upgrades are expected to close a crossing in north Marble Falls.

What residents should know

A November 25 report says the crossing will soon close for construction. The accessible summary does not specify the exact closure dates or detour. Drivers should consult city road notices before changing their route.
