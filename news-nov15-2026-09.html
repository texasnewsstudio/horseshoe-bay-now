BURNET WORKERS EARN COMMERCIAL DRIVING SKILLS THROUGH NONPROFIT

Published November 15, 2026 · Horseshoe Bay Now

A nonprofit program has helped Burnet employees obtain commercial driver's licenses.

Local reporting dated November 13 says a nonprofit focused on matching workforce skills with employer needs assisted several city of Burnet employees in earning CDLs. Such credentials can support public works and other essential municipal services.

Training programs can help local governments develop staff without relying entirely on outside recruitment. The report illustrates how workforce partnerships can address practical service needs in smaller communities.
