LLANO RIVER RISE LEAVES LOW-WATER CROSSINGS IMPASSABLE

Recent upstream rainfall pushed river levels higher, creating problems at some crossings.

HORSESHOE BAY NOW · Original source report: November 10, 2026

The Llano River rose several feet after rainfall across its upper basin, and local reporting noted that multiple low-water crossings became impassable on Tuesday, November 10. Conditions can change quickly after rain. Drivers should follow posted closures and check current road conditions before traveling.
