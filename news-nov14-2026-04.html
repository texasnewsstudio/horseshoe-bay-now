BURNET COUNTY SEEKS PUBLIC INPUT ON 20-YEAR ROAD PLAN

Three meetings will help shape future county roads, bridges and transportation investment.

HORSESHOE BAY NOW · Source reporting: DailyTrib · November 13

Burnet County commissioners are developing a long-range transportation infrastructure plan intended to guide road and bridge priorities for the next two decades.

Public meetings are scheduled for November 18 at Lakeside Pavilion in Marble Falls, December 1 at Bertram Community Center, and December 8 at Burnet Community Center, each from 5 to 7 p.m. Residents can offer input before priorities are finalized.
