WIRTZ DAM BRIDGE PROJECT DRAWS RENEWED ATTENTION

Published November 15, 2026 · Horseshoe Bay Now

The long-discussed Wirtz Dam bridge project is back in the regional conversation.

A November 12 local update revisited the bridge project near Lake LBJ. Transportation decisions around dam crossings can affect daily commutes, emergency access and how traffic moves between Highland Lakes communities.

The existence of an update does not mean construction has started or a final schedule has been approved. Residents should check transportation agency notices for milestones and any traffic restrictions.
