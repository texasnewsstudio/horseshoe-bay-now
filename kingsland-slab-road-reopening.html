Slab Road reopened following wet weather

The regional road crossing was reported reopened on November 23.

What residents should know

The report also noted additional rain in the forecast at that time. A reopening earlier this week does not guarantee that the crossing is open now. Drivers should obey barricades and check current official road conditions.
