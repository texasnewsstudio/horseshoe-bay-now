COTTONWOOD SHORES MOVES TOWARD WARNING SIREN SYSTEM

Published November 15, 2026 · Horseshoe Bay Now

A planned warning-siren installation could add another way to alert residents.

DailyTrib reported November 11 that Cottonwood Shores is installing a warning siren system. Outdoor sirens can supplement phone alerts and weather radios, particularly when fast-moving severe weather threatens an area.

A siren should not be a resident's only warning source. National Weather Service alerts, local emergency notifications and a weather radio remain important, especially indoors where sirens may be hard to hear.
