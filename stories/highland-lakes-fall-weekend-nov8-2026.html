FROM MUSIC TO MOTORCYCLES: HIGHLAND LAKES PACKS THE WEEKEND

November 8, 2026 · Horseshoe Bay Now News Desk

Burnet, Marble Falls and neighboring communities scheduled concerts, a motorcycle event and family activities for November 6–8.

The Highland Lakes entered November with a crowded weekend calendar. Regional listings for November 6–8 included live music, a motorcycle gathering, outdoor family activities and performances at local venues. Not every event ran all three days, but together they offered residents an unusually broad set of choices.

In Burnet, the Texas M/C Revival was scheduled for Saturday, November 7, from 10 a.m. to 4 p.m. The Hill Country Community Theatre listed a Two Tons of Steel performance that evening. In Marble Falls, Music on Main at Old Oak Square and seasonal activities at Sweet Berry Farm were among the attractions featured in the weekend guide.

The events also reveal the region’s geographic variety. A family could plan a farm visit in Marble Falls, a concert in Spicewood or a different kind of outing in Burnet. The distances between towns are manageable for many residents, but each location has its own character and audience.

One reason the calendar matters is seasonality. Summer brings lake traffic and vacationers, but fall events help businesses and attractions remain visible after the busiest water-recreation months. That does not mean every event delivers a measurable economic boost; attendance and spending data would be needed to make that claim.

Weather can also rewrite a weekend plan. Rain and cool temperatures are expected across the area Sunday, and anyone hoping to catch a final-day activity should check whether it is indoors, outdoors or subject to a revised schedule. A listing published earlier in the week may not reflect a last-minute change.

The larger picture is encouraging for people looking for things to do close to home. Rather than relying on a single major festival, the Highland Lakes calendar draws from arts organizations, local businesses, community venues and family attractions. That diversity gives residents options even when one event ends or the weather turns.

Reporting basis: Source and background information. Forecasts and event schedules may change.
