Hill Country Rain: What Residents Should Watch As The Week Begins

Rain and wet roads remain the main concern after a damp weekend, with conditions expected to improve as the system moves away.

After a wet Sunday, residents around Horseshoe Bay, Marble Falls and Burnet should keep a close eye on conditions at low-water crossings. Even when rainfall eases, runoff can linger in creeks and dips along local roads.

The National Weather Service outlook issued over the weekend called for repeated showers and some embedded thunderstorms through Monday, followed by improving conditions later in the week. Forecast timing varies from one community to another.

For residents and visitors, the practical advice is to check current radar and any active alerts before traveling, particularly on rural routes. A road that looks passable from a distance may have moving water across it.

This report summarizes the latest available regional forecast discussion; it is not a claim that a flood warning is currently in effect.
