Llano Isd Sets 2026–27 Tax Rate: Here Are The Numbers

The district adopted $0.7693 per $100 valuation, divided between operations and debt service.

Llano Independent School District says trustees adopted a 2026–27 property tax rate of $0.7693 per $100 of taxable value on August 24. The rate consists of $0.6519 for maintenance and operations and $0.1174 for interest and sinking. A school district rate is only one part of a property tax bill, and the amount paid depends on the property’s taxable value and exemptions. Residents should consult district documents and their county tax office for the details that apply to their properties.
