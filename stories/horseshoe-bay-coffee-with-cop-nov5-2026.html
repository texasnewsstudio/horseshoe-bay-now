Horseshoe Bay Police Host Coffee with a Cop Thursday Morning

Horseshoe Bay residents can meet patrol, investigations and dispatch officers at City Center Council Chambers from 9 to 10 a.m. Thursday.

The City of Horseshoe Bay Police Department is holding Coffee with a Cop on Thursday, November 5, from 9 to 10 a.m. at City Center Council Chambers, 9101 W. FM 2147. The free event gives residents a chance to speak informally with police officers about community concerns.

The city says the gathering will use separate tables for officers working in patrol, criminal investigations and dispatch. Residents may ask about traffic laws, fraud prevention, identity theft and neighborhood safety. Coffee and pastries will be available.

Residents may drop in during the hour. The city has asked attendees to RSVP at csharp@horseshoe-bay-tx.gov to help estimate attendance.
