Burnet Basketball Season Gets Underway

Burnet High School shifts toward basketball as football and cross-country seasons wind down.

The Burnet Bulldogs’ fall sports calendar is giving way to winter competition. A November 9 school-sports update reported that basketball seasons are gearing up while football and cross-country activities wind down.

Families looking for game times and locations should consult the school’s official athletics calendar, as schedules and venues can change. The transition gives students and supporters another round of local competition to follow.
