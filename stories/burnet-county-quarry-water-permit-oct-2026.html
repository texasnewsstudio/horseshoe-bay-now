Burnet County Quarry Groundwater Permit Faces Further Review

A groundwater permit associated with a proposed 715-acre rock quarry in Burnet County has been designated a contested matter by the local groundwater conservation district. The October 21 development opens the way for further hearings and independent review.

The distinction matters: the designation is not a final approval or rejection of the permit. Water availability and industrial development are issues that can affect communities well beyond the immediate project site.
