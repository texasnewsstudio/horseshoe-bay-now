Burnet Rolls Out New Trash Service And Residential Rates

The city says its November 1 transition includes weekly bulky waste and brush pickup and a $25 senior rate.

The City of Burnet says a transition to Texas Disposal Systems took effect November 1, with updated solid-waste services and rates. Its published residential rate is $29 per month for one 96-gallon trash cart and one 96-gallon recycling cart, with a $25 senior citizen rate. Weekly curbside collection of bulky waste and brush is included in the announced changes. Burnet residents should check city instructions for service rules and pickup schedules.
