Former Horseshoe Bay Mayor Steve Jordan Dies at 87

The Highland Lakes community remembers former Horseshoe Bay Mayor Steve Jordan, whose death was reported November 4.

Former Horseshoe Bay Mayor Steve Jordan has died at age 87, according to a November 4 report by Highland Lakes news outlet DailyTrib.

Jordan’s death marks the loss of a former local civic leader. The report places his passing among the major community stories of the week.

Horseshoe Bay Now extends condolences to his family and those who knew him. Readers seeking additional biographical details and memorial information should consult the original local reporting.
