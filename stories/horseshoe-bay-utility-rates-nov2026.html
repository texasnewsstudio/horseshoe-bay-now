Horseshoe Bay Utility Rate Changes Now in Effect

City utility rate changes took effect November 1; residents should check their next bill and the city’s published rate schedule.

Horseshoe Bay utility rate changes took effect November 1, 2026, according to the city’s official website and public notices.

Residents should use the official city rate information rather than assume a single increase applies to every customer.
