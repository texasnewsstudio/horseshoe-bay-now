New Hteao Location Brings Another Stop To Burnet

The tea chain opened its Burnet storefront over the weekend, adding a new option for local customers.

Burnet’s retail and restaurant scene gained another option over the November 6–8 weekend with the opening of a new HTeaO location. The grand opening was highlighted in local business coverage published November 6.

For residents, the opening adds another beverage stop in a community that serves both local shoppers and visitors traveling through the Highland Lakes. New storefronts also offer a visible sign of continued commercial activity in the area.

Customers should check the business directly for current operating hours, promotions and menu availability rather than relying on grand-opening schedules.

The opening was reported November 6; this is a November 9 follow-up, not a claim of a second opening today.
