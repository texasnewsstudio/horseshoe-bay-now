Granite Shoals Christmas Outreach Fundraiser Set For Saturday

A November 14 barbecue fundraiser will support a local holiday outreach effort.

Residents looking for a way to support local families this holiday season have an upcoming opportunity in Granite Shoals. Local event coverage lists a Christmas Outreach barbecue fundraiser for Saturday, November 14.

The event arrives as community groups across the Highland Lakes begin preparing seasonal assistance and holiday activities. Fundraisers like this one help connect local generosity with families and children who may need extra support.

Anyone planning to attend should confirm the latest event time, location and participation details with the organizers before heading out.

The event was listed in local coverage November 4; as of November 9 it remains an upcoming event.
