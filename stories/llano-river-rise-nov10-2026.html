Llano River Rise Expected After Upper-Basin Rain

Recent rainfall upstream could bring minor flooding along the Llano River Tuesday into Wednesday.

Several inches of rain across the upper Llano River basin have raised concern about rising river levels on November 10 and 11. The local forecast highlights the possibility of minor flooding, rather than a widespread disaster.

For people living or traveling near the Llano River, the distinction matters: a river can rise even after rain has ended at a particular location. Runoff takes time to travel downstream. Residents should avoid low-water crossings and check official river gauges and any active National Weather Service flood products before heading out.
