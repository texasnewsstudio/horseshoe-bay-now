Granite Springs Ranch Developers Outline 912-Acre Project Near Granite Shoals

Developers of the proposed Granite Springs Ranch subdivision are outlining plans for a 912-acre development and potential city annexation.

The proposed Granite Springs Ranch development is drawing attention around Granite Shoals. Reporting published November 5 describes plans for a 912-acre subdivision, with developers seeking annexation and a connection to the city water system. Those steps would involve public decision-making, and the proposal should not be mistaken for a completed or approved neighborhood. For residents around Lake LBJ, the questions include long-term water demand, road capacity and the timing of any municipal approvals.
