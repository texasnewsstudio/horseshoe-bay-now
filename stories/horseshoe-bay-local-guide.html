Horseshoe Bay and the Highland Lakes: A Local Information Guide

Horseshoe Bay Now original local information feature.

Horseshoe Bay sits in the Texas Hill Country near Lake LBJ, making it a convenient base for outdoor recreation and visits around the Highland Lakes.

For new residents and weekend visitors, useful information often includes local weather, road conditions, lake access, dining and community services. Keeping these resources in one place can save time.

Before planning a trip, confirm business hours, event schedules and any local restrictions with the responsible organization. Details can change, especially around holidays and busy weekends.

Horseshoe Bay Now aims to bring practical local information together with weather and community coverage. This permanent guide will remain available as newer local stories are published.
