Burnet Prepares for New Trash Collection Provider in November

The city of Burnet is preparing to change its waste collection provider to Texas Disposal Systems in November, according to an October 23 report. The transition affects Burnet city service rather than automatically changing service in Horseshoe Bay.

Burnet residents should watch for city instructions about schedules and collection procedures. Horseshoe Bay readers can use this coverage to follow important municipal changes elsewhere in the two-county area.
