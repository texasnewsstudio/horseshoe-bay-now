Marble Falls and Burnet Prepare for November 4 Food Drive

Tuesday, November 3, 2026 • Morning local news update

HIGHLAND LAKES, Texas — The Picayune and KBEY food drive is scheduled for Nov. 4 at both cities’ fire stations.

Two communities, one food drive

A Highland Lakes community food drive is scheduled for Wednesday, November 4, at fire stations in Marble Falls and Burnet. The annual effort is organized by The Picayune Magazine and KBEY 103.9 FM Radio Picayune, according to a local report published Monday, November 2.

When and where

Organizers have announced a collection window from 6 a.m. to 3 p.m. Wednesday at the Marble Falls and Burnet fire stations. The two-site format gives residents on either side of the Highland Lakes a nearby way to take part.

Planning to participate

Residents who want to donate should check the organizer’s latest announcement for the current list of requested items and any instructions before arriving. This report does not independently verify a specific list of accepted donations or the final distribution totals.
