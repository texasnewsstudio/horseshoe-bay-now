Llano County Precinct 4 Commissioner Jerry Don Moss Wins Reelection

Incumbent Jerry Don Moss defeated challenger Leila Green Little in the November 3 election, according to reported final precinct results.

Incumbent Republican Jerry Don Moss won the Llano County Precinct 4 commissioner election on November 3, according to election results reported by DailyTrib.

The reported tally was 2,131 votes for Moss, or 81.93 percent, and 470 for Democratic challenger Leila Green Little, or 18.07 percent.

The result keeps Moss in the Precinct 4 seat. As with other election-night reporting, readers can consult county election officials for certification and final canvass information.
