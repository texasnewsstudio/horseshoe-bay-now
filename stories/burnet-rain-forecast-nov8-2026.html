BURNET AND MARBLE FALLS FACE A COOL, RAINY START TO THE WEEK

November 8, 2026 · Horseshoe Bay Now News Desk

The Burnet-area forecast calls for highs in the low-to-mid 60s and substantial rain chances before conditions improve.

For residents of Burnet and Marble Falls, Sunday is shaping up as a day to keep the rain jacket close and outdoor plans flexible. The National Weather Service forecast issued early November 8 calls for widespread showers, occasional thunderstorms and temperatures struggling to climb much beyond the lower 60s.

Burnet Municipal Airport provides a useful local reference point. The weather service listed a forecast high near 62 degrees Sunday, followed by approximately 65 degrees Monday. Rain chances were high Sunday, with showers remaining possible Monday. Those are forecasts, not observations, and the timing of individual rain bands will vary across Burnet County.

The incoming upper-level system has been slow to move east, which helps explain the prolonged cloud cover. Moisture rising over cooler air can produce long periods of light or moderate rain, interrupted by more vigorous cells. The weather service specifically highlighted the possibility of heavier rain where thunderstorms become embedded in the larger precipitation area.

For schools, commuters and businesses, Monday morning deserves particular attention. Wet pavement and reduced visibility can add time to routine trips, especially before sunrise. Parents and workers should check local alerts and allow extra time rather than trying to predict exactly which road will be wettest.

The wet spell will also affect the weekend atmosphere in the lakes area. Restaurants and indoor attractions may see visitors adjusting plans, while outdoor recreation becomes more weather-dependent. Boaters should pay attention not only to rainfall but also to visibility and any lightning in the vicinity.

The outlook improves later in the week as the disturbance shifts away. The weather service expects conditions to become drier and somewhat milder by midweek. For now, the most useful forecast is a simple one: expect interruptions from rain, respect any flooded roadway, and check the latest local forecast before leaving home.
