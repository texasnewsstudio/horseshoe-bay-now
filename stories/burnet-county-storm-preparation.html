A Practical Storm Preparation Guide for Highland Lakes Homes

Horseshoe Bay Now original community information feature. This is general guidance, not a live news alert.

A few simple preparations can make severe weather less disruptive in the Highland Lakes area. Know where you would shelter during a tornado warning and how you would receive alerts overnight.

Keep a flashlight, charged phone or backup battery, water, medications, and basic supplies accessible. Move away from windows during severe storms and identify an interior room on the lowest practical floor.

Check the National Weather Service and county emergency-management information for current alerts and preparedness guidance. Review your plan before severe weather is in the forecast.
