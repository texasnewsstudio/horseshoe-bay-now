Election Day Arrives in Llano and Burnet Counties

Tuesday, November 3, 2026 • Morning local news update

HIGHLAND LAKES, Texas — Polls are scheduled to open from 7 a.m. to 7 p.m. Tuesday for the 2026 midterm election.

Polls open Tuesday

Tuesday, November 3, is Election Day for the 2026 midterm elections. Polling places in Llano and Burnet counties are scheduled to be open from 7 a.m. to 7 p.m. Local reporting published November 2 confirmed the hours and highlighted races affecting both counties.

Local decisions

Along with state and federal contests, Llano County voters will decide a Precinct 4 commissioner race. The races and ballot choices available to an individual voter depend on their registered address and voting precinct. Horseshoe Bay-area residents should check their registration county rather than assume every polling place offers the same ballot.

Before heading out

Voters can consult their county elections office or the Texas Secretary of State’s voting information website for official polling locations, acceptable identification and sample ballots. This morning report does not include vote totals or election results, which were not yet available when published.
