Horseshoe Bay Weather: Quiet Start, Then a Wetter Thursday and Friday

Horseshoe Bay Now Weather · Powered by Texas News Studio

Tuesday: Take advantage of the quiet weather

Here's the good news for Horseshoe Bay: Tuesday is a comfortable day to be outside. Expect a mix of sunshine and clouds, with temperatures around 80 degrees and a light southeast breeze. Tonight turns partly cloudy, with temperatures falling into the lower 60s. Whether you're heading toward Lake LBJ or squeezing in a late round of golf, the weather is largely on your side.

Wednesday: Mostly sunny, with a small late-day question mark

Wednesday looks like another warm day, with mostly sunny skies and temperatures reaching the lower 80s. The National Weather Service mentions only a slight possibility of a shower or thunderstorm late in the afternoon. That is not a forecast for widespread storms. Most of the day looks favorable for outdoor plans, but check radar if clouds begin building late.

Thursday and Friday: The unsettled part of the week

Now here's the stretch worth paying attention to. Clouds increase Thursday, and showers become more possible, especially later in the day. A thunderstorm cannot be ruled out. Friday looks cooler, with highs in the middle 70s and showers likely at times; a thunderstorm is possible as well.

That does not mean it will rain continuously at every home or marina. The important message is that Thursday and Friday have the best potential for wet weather this week. If you have boating, golf or outdoor work planned, keep a backup option and monitor updates.

Halloween weekend: Improving conditions

Saturday should bring some breaks in the clouds and temperatures returning to around 80 degrees. There is still a small possibility of a shower or thunderstorm, so check the forecast before Halloween activities. Sunday currently looks like the more dependable outdoor day, with mostly sunny skies and highs in the lower to middle 80s.

Early next week: Warm, with limited rain possibilities

Monday is forecast to be partly sunny and warm, again reaching the lower 80s, with a small possibility of showers. Forecast details that far out can change, so we won't pretend to know the exact timing of a system that has not developed yet.

What Horseshoe Bay residents should remember

Enjoy the quieter weather Tuesday and Wednesday. Pay closer attention to Thursday and Friday, when showers and a possible thunderstorm could affect travel, lake activities and golf. Conditions should generally improve over the weekend. If thunder is heard, move indoors and stay off the lake.
