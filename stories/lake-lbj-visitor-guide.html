Lake LBJ: A Practical Guide to Enjoying the Highland Lakes

Horseshoe Bay Now original local information feature.

Lake LBJ is one of the best-known recreation destinations in the Highland Lakes region of Central Texas. Around Horseshoe Bay, residents and visitors use the lake for boating, fishing and waterfront relaxation.

Before heading onto the water, check the local forecast and pay particular attention to thunderstorms and wind. Conditions on open water can change quickly, even when the morning looks calm.

Boaters should confirm current lake conditions, access rules and safety requirements through official local resources. Life jackets, a weather-aware plan and a designated return time are useful precautions.

For visitors staying in Horseshoe Bay, a lake outing can be combined with dining, sightseeing and other Hill Country activities. This guide is intended as an evergreen starting point, not a real-time report of lake conditions.
