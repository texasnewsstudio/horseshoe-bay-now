Highland Lakes Locals Love Us Voting Opens for 2027 Honors

Residents can vote for their favorite area businesses and services through November 30.

The 2027 Locals Love Us contest is underway, inviting Highland Lakes residents to recognize favorite businesses and services. Organized by The Picayune and KBEY 103.9 FM, the annual community contest is accepting votes through November 30, according to a November 4 announcement. Residents interested in participating can find voting information through the organizers.
