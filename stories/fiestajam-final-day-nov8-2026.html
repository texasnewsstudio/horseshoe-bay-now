FIESTAJAM REACHES ITS FINAL DAY ACROSS THE HIGHLAND LAKES

November 8, 2026 · Horseshoe Bay Now News Desk

The November 6–8 songwriter and concert weekend offers a reminder of the region’s growing live-music calendar.

The Highland Lakes music weekend reaches its scheduled final day Sunday, November 8, as FiestaJAM wraps a three-day run of performances and songwriter-focused events. The festival is part of a busy early-November calendar that has brought visitors and local audiences to venues across the area.

Organizers and regional event listings scheduled FiestaJAM for November 6 through November 8. The published weekend lineup also included Diamond Rio at Round Mountain Cider Mill on Friday and Paul Overstreet at Spicewood Live! that evening. Those performances were advertised for earlier in the weekend and should not be mistaken for Sunday-night events.

Events like FiestaJAM matter beyond the stage. They bring attention to smaller communities that can otherwise be overshadowed by Austin’s entertainment scene. Visitors may combine a show with a meal, a hotel stay or a drive through the lakes area. The effect on individual businesses varies, but the wider opportunity is clear: live music gives the region another reason to attract people outside peak summer boating season.

The songwriter format also offers something different from a conventional concert. Rather than focusing only on familiar hits, songwriter showcases invite audiences to consider the people and stories behind the music. That can make a smaller venue feel unusually personal, especially when artists explain how a song came together.

Sunday’s weather is an important complication. The National Weather Service expects a damp, cool day with periodic showers across much of South Central Texas. Anyone planning to attend a remaining performance should confirm the venue, ticket status and any schedule changes directly with the organizer before setting out.

The broader Highland Lakes event calendar remains active after the festival, but individual programs should be checked for current details. For residents, the takeaway from this weekend is that the local arts scene continues to offer more than a seasonal diversion: it is becoming part of the region’s year-round identity.
