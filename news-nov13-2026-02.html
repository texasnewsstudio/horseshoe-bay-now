COTTONWOOD SHORES MOVES AHEAD WITH OUTDOOR WARNING SIRENS

The city is installing two sirens intended to help alert residents during severe weather and other emergencies.

HORSESHOE BAY NOW · Original source report: November 11, 2026

Cottonwood Shores is working to install two outdoor warning sirens. The equipment is intended to provide another way to alert people outdoors during tornadoes, dangerous storms and other emergencies. Sirens are one part of an alert system; residents should also have a way to receive phone or weather-radio warnings.
