BURNET VOLLEYBALL COACH EARNS COACH OF THE YEAR HONOR

Crystal Shipley is recognized after a record-setting season for the Lady Dawgs.

Burnet High School volleyball coach Crystal Shipley has received a Coach of the Year honor following a record-breaking 2026 season, according to reporting published November 11. The recognition highlights a strong year for the Lady Dawgs and adds another achievement to the school's fall sports season.
