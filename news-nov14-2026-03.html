HILL COUNTRY FACES WET WEEKEND BEFORE COOLER AIR ARRIVES

Rain spreads across Central Texas Saturday and Sunday, with cooler conditions early next week.

HORSESHOE BAY NOW · Source reporting: NWS Austin/San Antonio · November 14

The National Weather Service Austin/San Antonio forecast discussion issued Saturday morning calls for showers and isolated thunderstorms spreading east across South-Central Texas, with additional rain Sunday.

LCRA meteorologist Bob Rose expects the Hill Country to see rain this weekend before cooler, drier conditions arrive Monday and Tuesday. Severe weather risk is more focused toward the Coastal Plains; localized downpours remain possible. Check the latest forecast before travel.
