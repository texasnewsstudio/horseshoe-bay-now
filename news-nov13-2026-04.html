BURNET WARNS RESIDENTS ABOUT SUSPICIOUS MAIL

City officials are cautioning residents about letters seeking additional payments.

HORSESHOE BAY NOW · Original source report: November 10, 2026

Burnet officials recently warned residents about a mail scam that appeared to demand additional payments. Anyone receiving an unexpected notice should verify it through the city's official contact channels rather than sending money or personal information to an unverified address.
