MARBLE FALLS HOUSING AUTHORITY PROPOSAL DIVIDES CITY LEADERS

A proposed city-sponsored housing authority drew disagreement among council members.

A proposal to create a housing authority in Marble Falls has prompted debate at City Council, according to November 12 reporting. Supporters and skeptics may weigh affordability, governance and the city's role in housing differently. The discussion is not the same as a final vote creating an authority, and the next steps depend on the city's formal process.
