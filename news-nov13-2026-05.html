BURNET VOLLEYBALL COACH EARNS COACH OF THE YEAR HONOR

Crystal Shipley receives recognition following a standout season for Burnet volleyball.

HORSESHOE BAY NOW · Original source report: November 11, 2026

Burnet volleyball coach Crystal Shipley has been recognized with a Coach of the Year honor following a record-breaking season. The award reflects a strong year for the program and gives the community another reason to celebrate local student-athletes and their coaches.
