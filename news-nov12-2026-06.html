BERTRAM VETERAN PARTNERS WITH META ON AWARENESS PROJECT

Jerry LeBlanc shared his experience with vision loss in a Veterans Day collaboration.

Bertram-area veteran and animal-rescue founder Jerry LeBlanc partnered with Meta for a Veterans Day awareness effort, local media reported November 11. The project highlights his experience with partial vision loss and technology's potential role in supporting veterans. The collaboration also brings a Highland Lakes resident's story to a wider audience.

Source: DailyTrib.com local reporting, November 10–11, 2026.
