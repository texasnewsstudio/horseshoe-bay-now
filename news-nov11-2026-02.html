BURNET WARNS RESIDENTS ABOUT MISLEADING MAIL

Burnet officials have alerted residents to mail solicitations seeking additional payments. Verify notices directly with the city.

The city of Burnet has warned residents about a mail scam involving demands for additional payments. Anyone receiving an unexpected payment request should verify it using the city's independently published contact information rather than phone numbers printed on the notice. The warning was reported November 10.
