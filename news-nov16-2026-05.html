COTTONWOOD SHORES SEEKS UP TO $750,000 FOR INFRASTRUCTURE

The city is pursuing state grant support for public infrastructure needs.

Cottonwood Shores is seeking up to $750,000 in state grant funding for infrastructure improvements, according to a November 10 report. The request reflects the cost pressures smaller lake communities face as they maintain roads and public systems. Applying for a grant does not guarantee an award; the amount and scope will depend on the state review process.
