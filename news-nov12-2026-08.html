FIESTAJAM ORGANIZERS AIM TO GROW MARBLE FALLS MUSIC IDENTITY

After the November festival, organizers are talking about a bigger songwriter presence.

Following the November 6–8 FiestaJAM celebration, organizers have expressed an ambition to make Marble Falls a recognized songwriter destination. Local reporting on November 10 described the event as part of a larger effort to strengthen the Highland Lakes music scene. The goal is aspirational; it is not an official city designation.

Source: DailyTrib.com local reporting, November 10–11, 2026.
