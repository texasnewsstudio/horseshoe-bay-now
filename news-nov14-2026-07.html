MARBLE FALLS COUNCIL SPLIT OVER HOUSING AUTHORITY IDEA

A proposed city-sponsored housing authority draws disagreement among council members.

HORSESHOE BAY NOW · Source reporting: DailyTrib · November 12

A proposal to establish a city-sponsored housing authority prompted debate on the Marble Falls City Council, according to November 12 reporting.

The discussion reflects pressure around housing and growth in the Highland Lakes. A debated proposal is not the same as a final approval; the source report outlines the disagreement.
