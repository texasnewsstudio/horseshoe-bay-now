PICKLEBALL SHUTDOWN! COURTS OFF-LIMITS THROUGH MONDAY

A city alert says Horseshoe Bay's pickleball courts are temporarily closed November 18–23.

Horseshoe Bay issued a November 17 public alert advising that city pickleball courts would be closed from Wednesday, November 18, through Monday, November 23.

Players planning weekend matches should avoid assuming courts have reopened early. The city's notice sets out the closure window, but does not itself establish conditions at every hour.

Check the city alert channel for changes before heading to the courts.
