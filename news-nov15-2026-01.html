MARBLE FALLS ARRESTS PUT REGIONAL WARRANT OPERATIONS IN FOCUS

Published November 15, 2026 · Horseshoe Bay Now

Three people were arrested in Marble Falls during a warrant operation reported Friday.

DailyTrib reported that three men were arrested on outstanding warrants on November 12 at a Marble Falls residence. The operation highlights how regional law-enforcement agencies can coordinate when individuals are wanted on charges across jurisdictions.

An arrest is not a conviction. The legal process will determine the outcome of each case. Residents looking for official details should consult law-enforcement announcements and court records rather than assume allegations have been proven.
