SECOND-CHANCE HIRING STIRS DEBATE! BERTRAM DEFENDS POLICE RECRUITS

The city asks residents to give two recently hired officers a chance amid questions about their employment histories.

The Bertram Police Department has hired two officers who reportedly were terminated from previous law-enforcement positions, according to local reporting published November 17.

The city has asked the public to give the officers a second chance. The reported employment histories have prompted scrutiny, but the mere fact of a prior termination does not establish criminal wrongdoing.

Residents seeking more detail should review the underlying reporting and any official statements rather than assume allegations have been proven.
