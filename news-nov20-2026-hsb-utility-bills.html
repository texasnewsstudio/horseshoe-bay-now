BILL SHOCK AHEAD? HORSESHOE BAY RESIDENTS FACE DECEMBER UTILITY CHANGES

Residents are being alerted to higher utility bills after city rate changes took effect November 1.

Horseshoe Bay utility rate changes took effect November 1, 2026. That means December bills may reflect new charges for city services, depending on billing periods and household usage.

The practical move is to compare the next statement with the city's current published rate schedule. A larger bill is not necessarily explained by rates alone: consumption and billing dates also matter.

This is a November 20 follow-up to previously announced changes, not a claim that a new rate increase was voted on today.
