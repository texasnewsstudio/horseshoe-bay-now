MARBLE FALLS HOUSING AUTHORITY PROPOSAL SPARKS DEBATE

Published November 15, 2026 · Horseshoe Bay Now

A proposed city-sponsored housing authority has divided Marble Falls council members.

Local reporting dated November 12 describes disagreement over whether Marble Falls should pursue a housing authority. The question touches on affordability, city responsibilities and how a growing community plans for future housing demand.

A proposal being debated is not an approved program. Residents should follow council agendas and minutes for the precise scope of any measure and its eventual outcome.
