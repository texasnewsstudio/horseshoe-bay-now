HORSESHOE BAY NOW DAILY WEATHER UPDATE RULES

1. Obtain and verify the current NWS Austin/San Antonio point forecast for Horseshoe Bay before writing. Do not rely on generic weather feeds.
2. Write lively, original, locally useful weather prose, but never invent model analysis, warnings, storms, observations, or credentials.
3. Do not include rain percentages in the editorial forecast. Describe timing, confidence, and trends accurately.
4. The homepage green card displays ONLY the latest daily forecast; replace its text on the next update.
5. Each dated forecast is a permanent standalone page. Never overwrite or delete older forecast pages; keep them linked under WEATHER > Local Weather News and News Archive and in sitemap.xml.
6. Keep county news links in the NEWS dropdown only; do not add Llano or Burnet County news cards to the homepage.
7. Preserve all existing site features, layout, weather maps, articles, and links.
8. Label weather as Horseshoe Bay Now, powered by Texas News Studio; do not claim personal analysis unless verified.
